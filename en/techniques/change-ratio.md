## Extend the scene to a new shape

[Smart Outpaint](https://podplo.app/skills/smart-outpaint) extends a complete scene into a wider, taller, or more spacious image.

[Smart Outpaint](https://podplo.app/skills/smart-outpaint) adds more sky, landscape, interior space, or atmosphere around a complete scene without stretching the subject. The image can become wider, taller, or simply more spacious.

## Rebuild the layout for a new shape

[Poster Maker](https://podplo.app/skills/poster-maker) rearranges the same artwork and copy for a new canvas shape.

[Poster Maker](https://podplo.app/skills/poster-maker) repositions the title, subject, and supporting line for a new shape instead of simply cropping the old image. The text and subject stay the same while the hierarchy changes.

## Rework the design as a compact emblem

[Compact Center Layout](https://podplo.app/skills/compact-center-layout) turns loose artwork and copy into a clean, compact emblem.

[Compact Center Layout](https://podplo.app/skills/compact-center-layout) gathers a subject and copy in a compact transparent composition rather than adding more scenery. A wide or loose design becomes a clear emblem-like graphic.
