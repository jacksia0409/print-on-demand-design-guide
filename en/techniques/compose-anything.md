## Build a strong centered graphic

<a id="skills-solve-structure"></a>

<a id="transparent-layout-family"></a>

<a id="opaque-artwork"></a>

[Compact Center Layout](https://podplo.app/skills/compact-center-layout) combines a recognizable subject and copy in one bold centered graphic.

Start with an uploaded image or a PodPlo asset. [Compact Center Layout](https://podplo.app/skills/compact-center-layout) combines the subject and copy in a balanced centered graphic while keeping the source recognizable.

### Build a strong centered graphic

[Compact Center Layout](https://podplo.app/skills/compact-center-layout) combines a subject and copy in a dense, balanced shape. A loose source image becomes a strong centered graphic with a clear focal point.

### Stack a tall subject and copy

[Vertical Stack Layout](https://podplo.app/skills/vertical-stack-layout) arranges a tall subject and copy from top to bottom in a clear reading order. The open composition leaves the background visible around the artwork.

### Keep the full scene intact

[Poster Maker](https://podplo.app/skills/poster-maker) can preserve a complete scene, frame, or texture when the surroundings are part of the design. The result reads as a finished poster rather than an isolated subject.

## Turn one motif into a repeat

<a id="choose-fewer-skills"></a>

[Seamless Pattern](https://podplo.app/skills/seamless-pattern) turns one small motif into a continuous repeat.

[Seamless Pattern](https://podplo.app/skills/seamless-pattern) repeats one motif with varied spacing, scale, and direction. A flower, icon, or character becomes a continuous pattern while the original shape remains recognizable.

### Compare an emblem with an open layout

[Circular Badge Layout](https://podplo.app/skills/circular-badge-layout) encloses the artwork and copy in a complete emblem, while Compact Center Layout creates a denser open shape. The same source can feel classic and contained or more energetic and informal.

## Turn one theme into a graphic set

<a id="verify-the-output"></a>

Develop a character or celebration into a matching collection, with each graphic ready to use on its own.

[Sticker & Clipart Pack](https://podplo.app/skills/sticker-clipart-pack) expands a character, hobby, or celebration into several coordinated standalone graphics. Each piece works on its own, and the full set remains visually consistent.

### Preview the design in context

Place the selected graphic in a simple product scene to see how color, object shape, and surroundings affect it. The same design can read as bold, understated, playful, or nostalgic in different settings.
