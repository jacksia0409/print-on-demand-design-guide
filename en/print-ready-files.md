## Bring out fine detail

[HD Upscale](https://podplo.app/skills/hd-upscale) sharpens texture and small details while preserving the composition.

[HD Upscale](https://podplo.app/skills/hd-upscale) can sharpen fine edges, textures, and small forms in a selected image. It adds clarity without changing the subject or composition.

## Reuse a subject on new backgrounds

[Background Remover](https://podplo.app/skills/background-remover) isolates a subject so it can be reused across different layouts and scenes.

[Background Remover](https://podplo.app/skills/background-remover) isolates a portrait, flower, or object so it can be used with new colors, layouts, and scenes. One photo becomes a reusable design element for several compositions.

## Extend the scene beyond the frame

[Smart Outpaint](https://podplo.app/skills/smart-outpaint) extends a photo into a wider, taller, or more spacious composition.

[Smart Outpaint](https://podplo.app/skills/smart-outpaint) adds plausible sky, landscape, interior space, or atmosphere around the original scene without stretching the subject. The same photo can become a wider composition, a taller one, or a roomier backdrop.
