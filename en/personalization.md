## Build the design around personal details

Names, dates, photos, colors, and short messages make a shared layout personal.

A name, date, number, photo, pet, color, or short message can make a design specific to one person. Use the details the customer gives you rather than filling in missing information.

## Use clear photos and confirmed details

Clear photos and confirmed details produce more specific, believable personalized artwork.

A clearer photo, an uncropped face, a confirmed date, or a shorter message will usually produce a stronger design. If a personal fact is missing, leave it out instead of asking the artwork to guess.
