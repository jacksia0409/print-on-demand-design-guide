## Choose what leads the image

A prominent subject can have a patterned ground and remain subject-led. Recognizable geometric or stylized subjects also stay in that family; nonfigurative compositions belong to abstraction. Explore text, lettering and background design for all five artwork forms below.

### Semantic relationships between text and subject
