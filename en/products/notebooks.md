# Notebooks and planners

Turn a notebook cover into a poster, a pattern, or a personalized design.

<a id="product-overview"></a>

## Design ideas for this product

### Start with the cover

Use one strong image, combine a title with the illustration, or extend a repeat across the entire cover. A centered cover image; A title integrated into the illustration; An all-over repeat.
