# Hats and caps

Turn a character, symbol, or short word into a compact hat graphic.

<a id="product-overview"></a>

## Design ideas for this product

### Keep it simple and recognizable

Clear silhouettes, compact badges, and short lettering work best on the small curved front of a hat. A centered mascot badge; A short word with distinctive lettering; A simple symbol with a strong outline.

## Preview the design on the product

### Preview it on the hat

The AI preview shows how the graphic looks at hat scale and on a curved front panel.

- One strong shape is easier to read than many small details

- A short name can be built into the badge

- The hat color can make the same graphic feel bold or understated
