# Tumblers

Adapt one image into a tall centerpiece, an all-over repeat, or a full wrap for a tumbler.

<a id="product-overview"></a>

## Design ideas for this product

### Use the height and the full wrap

Tall characters, stacked type, scattered motifs, and panoramic scenes all work well on a tumbler. A tall central character; Small motifs scattered from top to bottom; A panoramic scene around the tumbler.
