# Phone cases

Create a vertical phone case design from a portrait, repeat pattern, or personalized graphic.

<a id="product-overview"></a>

## Design ideas for this product

### Use the vertical format

Phone cases work well with tall subjects, scattered motifs, and personal details built into the artwork. A portrait framed by small symbols; An all-over repeat; A name integrated into the graphic.
