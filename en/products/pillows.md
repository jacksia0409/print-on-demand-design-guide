# Pillows and cushions

Use the square pillow format for a centered image, a short phrase, or a repeat pattern.

<a id="product-overview"></a>

## Design ideas for this product

### Build around the square

Centered subjects, symmetrical layouts, and balanced repeats all work well on a pillow. A centered portrait; A short phrase used as the main graphic; A repeat designed to coordinate with other pillows.
