# Puzzles

Create puzzle artwork from a detailed scene, family photo, or illustrated world.

<a id="product-overview"></a>

## Design ideas for this product

### Fill the image with useful detail

Faces, color changes, and distinct objects give each area of the puzzle something recognizable. A detailed illustrated scene; A family portrait with personal objects; A scene with distinct visual landmarks.
