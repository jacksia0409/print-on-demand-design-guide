# Pet feeding mats

Design a pet feeding mat around a portrait, a name, and a set of related motifs.

<a id="product-overview"></a>

## Design ideas for this product

### Make the pet the main subject

Place the portrait beside the bowls, repeat a familiar symbol, or build the pet’s name into the design. A portrait placed to one side; A name paired with paws and favorite objects; A repeat arranged around the bowl area.

## Preview the design on the product

### Preview the design with the bowls in place

The AI preview shows the placement of the pet portrait, name, and motifs around the bowls.

- Place the portrait to one side to keep the center open

- Use the name to connect the surrounding motifs

- A repeat gives the mat a consistent look from every angle
