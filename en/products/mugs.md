# Mugs

Use the full mug: feature an image on one side, add a phrase on the other, or wrap a repeat around the cup.

<a id="product-overview"></a>

## Design ideas for this product

### Design beyond the front

A mug can pair images and words across two sides or carry one continuous design around the cup. A portrait paired with a short phrase; Related graphics on opposite sides; A repeating pattern around the mug.
