# Wrapping paper

Repeat one motif across a full sheet of wrapping paper, then add holiday details or personalized elements.

<a id="product-overview"></a>

## Design ideas for this product

### Build the design from one repeat

Characters, icons, and personal details can be repeated at different scales to create a coordinated sheet. A loose repeat with more open space; A dense repeat of holiday icons; A name repeated within the pattern.

## Preview the design on the product

### Preview it on a wrapped box

The AI preview shows how the same repeat creates different moments across the top, sides, and folds of a wrapped box.

- A loose repeat leaves more open space

- A dense repeat keeps the pattern visible across folds

- A name can repeat at the same scale as the other motifs
