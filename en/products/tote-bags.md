# Tote bags

Use the front of a tote for a bold centered graphic, a short message, or a repeat pattern.

<a id="product-overview"></a>

## Design ideas for this product

### A design people can carry

Centered artwork, large lettering, and compact groups of motifs all fit the tote’s broad front panel. A bold centered graphic; A short, readable message; A group of objects from one theme.

## Preview the design on the product

### Preview it on the tote

Use the AI preview to compare a bold statement graphic with a simpler everyday design.

- A centered image creates a balanced layout

- Large type makes the message easy to read

- A smaller motif is easier to pair with different outfits
