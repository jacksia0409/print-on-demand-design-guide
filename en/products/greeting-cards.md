# Greeting cards and invitations

Create a greeting card around an illustration, a short message, or a personalized name and date.

<a id="product-overview"></a>

## Design ideas for this product

### Design for a specific occasion

The front of the card can lead with the image, the message, or a balanced combination of both. A warm illustration with a simple message; A playful image built around a joke; A name or date added to the occasion.
