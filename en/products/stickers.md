# Stickers and labels

Turn one idea into a character sticker, a reaction phrase, or a matching set.

<a id="product-overview"></a>

## Design ideas for this product

### Small format, big personality

Use a clear character, bold symbol, or group of related graphics that can work alone or as a set. A mascot with a clear expression; A short reaction phrase; A small set of related icons.
