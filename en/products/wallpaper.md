## Design ideas for this product

### Choose the scale and spacing

Small dense motifs create a subtle background, larger spaced motifs stand out across the wall, and vertical or diagonal repeats guide the eye through the room. A small repeat for a subtle background; A large spaced motif for a statement wall; A vertical or diagonal repeat.
