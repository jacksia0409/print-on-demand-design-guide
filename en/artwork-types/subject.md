# Subject-led

An identifiable object, a group or lettering leads the design. Explore composition, supporting decoration and text relationships.

<a id="subject-design"></a>

## Subject composition and treatments

<a id="transformation"></a>

VISUAL FORMS · 144

### Subject art treatments

<a id="transformation-panel-word-g22"></a>

<a id="transformation-group-word-g22"></a>

#### Contours and lines

Compare how contours are extracted, minimized, or loosened into scribbles while the underlying subject remains recognizable.

<a id="form-art-22-0"></a>

![Contour extraction — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-22-0.svg)

##### Contour extraction

Isolate the subject’s outer edges into decisive lines, removing surface detail while preserving its recognizable profile.

<a id="form-art-22-2"></a>

![Minimal line drawing — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-22-2.svg)

##### Minimal line drawing

Describe the subject with only a few economical strokes, leaving open space to carry most of the form.

<a id="form-art-22-7"></a>

![Scribbled lines — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-22-7.svg)

##### Scribbled lines

Build the image from restless, overlapping marks whose irregular motion gives the subject nervous energy.

<a id="transformation-panel-word-g23"></a>

<a id="transformation-group-word-g23"></a>

#### Reduction and simplification

Compare strategies for removing detail while preserving identity, spanning omission, silhouettes, icons, symbols, and simplified value structure.

<a id="form-art-23-0"></a>

![Remove background — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-23-0.svg)

##### Remove background

Erase the surrounding setting so the subject reads as a clean, self-contained graphic shape.

<a id="form-art-23-1"></a>

![Remove minor details — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-23-1.svg)

##### Remove minor details

Suppress small textures and secondary parts, allowing the essential structure to become immediately legible.

<a id="form-art-23-2"></a>

![Retain identifying features — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-23-2.svg)

##### Retain identifying features

Preserve the few proportions, markings, or accessories that make the subject unmistakably itself.

<a id="form-art-23-3"></a>

![Selective omission — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-23-3.svg)

##### Selective omission

Intentionally leave chosen areas unresolved, directing attention through controlled gaps rather than complete rendering.

<a id="form-art-23-4"></a>

![Negative-space completion — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-23-4.svg)

##### Negative-space completion

Let empty areas imply missing edges, so viewers mentally complete a form that is never fully drawn.

<a id="form-art-23-5"></a>

![Silhouette reduction — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-23-5.svg)

##### Silhouette reduction

Compress the subject into a solid outline whose pose and profile carry all recognition.

<a id="form-art-23-6"></a>

![Icon reduction — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-23-6.svg)

##### Icon reduction

Reduce the subject to a compact pictogram with bold geometry and instant small-scale readability.

<a id="form-art-23-7"></a>

![Symbol reduction — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-23-7.svg)

##### Symbol reduction

Convert the idea into an emblematic sign, favoring conceptual recognition over literal appearance.

<a id="form-art-23-8"></a>

![Color-block simplification — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-23-8.svg)

##### Color-block simplification

Replace gradual modeling with a few broad, interlocking regions of flat color.

<a id="form-art-23-9"></a>

![Value grouping — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-23-9.svg)

##### Value grouping

Organize light and dark into a small number of clear tonal masses instead of continuous shading.

<a id="form-art-23-10"></a>

![Simplified contour — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-23-10.svg)

##### Simplified contour

Smooth irregular edges into an economical contour that retains the subject’s defining silhouette.

<a id="form-art-23-11"></a>

![Simplified inner structure — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-23-11.svg)

##### Simplified inner structure

Keep only major internal divisions, removing minor anatomy, texture, and construction lines.

<a id="transformation-group-word-g24"></a>

#### Proportion and shape

Compare deliberate changes to proportion, volume, curvature, compression, torsion, and lens-like deformation of a recognizable form.

<a id="form-art-24-0"></a>

![Oversized head — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-24-0.svg)

##### Oversized head

Enlarge the head far beyond natural proportion to emphasize expression, innocence, or comic personality.

<a id="form-art-24-1"></a>

![Elongated body — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-24-1.svg)

##### Elongated body

Stretch the torso and overall silhouette vertically, producing an elegant or deliberately uncanny figure.

<a id="form-art-24-2"></a>

![Shortened limbs — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-24-2.svg)

##### Shortened limbs

Compress arms or legs into stubby proportions that make the body feel compact and playful.

<a id="form-art-24-3"></a>

![Enlarged body detail — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-24-3.svg)

##### Enlarged body detail

Magnify one anatomical feature so it becomes the character’s dominant visual cue.

<a id="form-art-24-4"></a>

![Reduced body detail — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-24-4.svg)

##### Reduced body detail

Shrink a selected feature to create contrast, vulnerability, or an intentionally odd proportion.

<a id="form-art-24-5"></a>

![Rounded forms — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-24-5.svg)

##### Rounded forms

Soften corners and joints into plump curves, giving every mass a friendly, cushioned appearance.

<a id="form-art-24-6"></a>

![Slender forms — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-24-6.svg)

##### Slender forms

Narrow widths and taper forms to create a lean, delicate, upward-moving silhouette.

<a id="form-art-24-7"></a>

![Flattened forms — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-24-7.svg)

##### Flattened forms

Compress depth until volumes read like pressed, shallow shapes with broad frontal surfaces.

<a id="form-art-24-8"></a>

![Inflated forms — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-24-8.svg)

##### Inflated forms

Expand volumes outward as if filled with air, rounding edges and tightening the silhouette.

<a id="form-art-24-9"></a>

![Squashed forms — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-24-9.svg)

##### Squashed forms

Press forms downward or sideways, creating squat proportions and visible compression.

<a id="form-art-24-10"></a>

![Bent forms — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-24-10.svg)

##### Bent forms

Curve normally rigid parts at clear joints, introducing posture, tension, or cartoon elasticity.

<a id="form-art-24-11"></a>

![Twisted forms — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-24-11.svg)

##### Twisted forms

Rotate connected parts in opposing directions so the form appears wrung or torsionally stressed.

<a id="form-art-24-12"></a>

![Wavy deformation — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-24-12.svg)

##### Wavy deformation

Undulate the entire outline in smooth alternating curves, replacing straight structure with flowing motion.

<a id="form-art-24-13"></a>

![Exaggerated perspective — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-24-13.svg)

##### Exaggerated perspective

Push near forms dramatically larger and distant forms smaller to intensify spatial depth.

<a id="form-art-24-14"></a>

![Fisheye distortion — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-24-14.svg)

##### Fisheye distortion

Bow straight lines around a rounded field of view, creating a wide-angle, lens-like bulge.

#### Characterization

Compare how subjects become characters through anthropomorphism, expression, limbs, clothing, occupation, personality, heroism, monstrosity, or mechanical life.

<a id="form-art-25-0"></a>

![Anthropomorphic animal — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-25-0.svg)

##### Anthropomorphic animal

Give an animal human posture, gesture, clothing, or social behavior while retaining its species features.

<a id="form-art-25-1"></a>

![Anthropomorphic object — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-25-1.svg)

##### Anthropomorphic object

Animate an everyday object with a face and bodily gestures derived from its original construction.

<a id="form-art-25-2"></a>

![Anthropomorphic food — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-25-2.svg)

##### Anthropomorphic food

Turn food into a character by adding expressive features without losing its appetizing recognizable form.

<a id="form-art-25-3"></a>

![Anthropomorphic plant — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-25-3.svg)

##### Anthropomorphic plant

Use stems, leaves, or blossoms as a living body with human emotion and purposeful movement.

<a id="form-art-25-4"></a>

![Added expression — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-25-4.svg)

##### Added expression

Add eyes, mouth, or brows that clearly communicate a chosen mood through economical facial cues.

<a id="form-art-25-5"></a>

![Added limbs — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-25-5.svg)

##### Added limbs

Attach arms or legs that let a nonhuman subject pose, act, and interact like a character.

<a id="form-art-25-6"></a>

![Dressed character — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-25-6.svg)

##### Dressed character

Use clothing to suggest role, era, status, or temperament while keeping the underlying figure readable.

<a id="form-art-25-7"></a>

![Occupational character — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-25-7.svg)

##### Occupational character

Define the character through profession-specific dress, tools, stance, and working behavior.

<a id="form-art-25-8"></a>

![Personality character — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-25-8.svg)

##### Personality character

Build a consistent temperament through posture, expression, styling, and repeated behavioral details.

<a id="form-art-25-9"></a>

![Heroic character — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-25-9.svg)

##### Heroic character

Broaden the stance, sharpen the gaze, and use upward gestures to convey courage and resolve.

<a id="form-art-25-10"></a>

![Monster character — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-25-10.svg)

##### Monster character

Distort anatomy into threatening or strange forms, balancing fear with memorable character logic.

<a id="form-art-25-11"></a>

![Mechanical lifeform — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-25-11.svg)

##### Mechanical lifeform

Combine machinery with signs of consciousness, making joints, sensors, and armor feel like living anatomy.

#### Deconstruction and assembly

Compare how a subject is divided, displaced, mirrored, modularized, and reconstructed while its assembly logic stays visible.

<a id="form-art-26-0"></a>

![Geometric decomposition — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-26-0.svg)

##### Geometric decomposition

Break the subject into basic polygons or solids that reveal an underlying constructed geometry.

<a id="form-art-26-1"></a>

![Layered slices — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-26-1.svg)

##### Layered slices

Divide the form into parallel cross-sections, separating them slightly to expose layered volume.

<a id="form-art-26-2"></a>

![Reassembled fragments — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-26-2.svg)

##### Reassembled fragments

Rebuild a recognizable whole from displaced shards whose seams remain visibly active.

<a id="form-art-26-3"></a>

![Joined planes — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-26-3.svg)

##### Joined planes

Connect angular surfaces edge to edge, letting planar facets define volume instead of smooth modeling.

<a id="form-art-26-4"></a>

![Local displacement — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-26-4.svg)

##### Local displacement

Shift one region away from its original alignment while the rest of the subject remains stable.

<a id="form-art-26-5"></a>

![Multiple-view assembly — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-26-5.svg)

##### Multiple-view assembly

Combine front, side, and top observations in one figure rather than obeying a single viewpoint.

<a id="form-art-26-6"></a>

![Repeated overlays — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-26-6.svg)

##### Repeated overlays

Stack translucent or offset copies so accumulated contours create depth and visual vibration.

<a id="form-art-26-7"></a>

![Mirrored assembly — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-26-7.svg)

##### Mirrored assembly

Join reflected halves or repeated mirror units into a deliberately artificial composite.

<a id="form-art-26-8"></a>

![Symmetrical reconstruction — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-26-8.svg)

##### Symmetrical reconstruction

Reconstruct the subject around a central axis, balancing corresponding parts without making them identical.

<a id="form-art-26-9"></a>

![Modular assembly — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-26-9.svg)

##### Modular assembly

Assemble the image from reusable units whose joints and repeated logic remain apparent.

<a id="form-art-26-10"></a>

![Part replacement — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-26-10.svg)

##### Part replacement

Swap one component for an unexpected substitute that preserves placement while changing meaning.

<a id="form-art-26-11"></a>

![Reconstruction with missing parts — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-26-11.svg)

##### Reconstruction with missing parts

Keep the larger structure recognizable despite deliberate gaps where essential components should be.

<a id="form-art-26-12"></a>

![Exploded assembly — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-26-12.svg)

##### Exploded assembly

Suspend separated parts around their original positions to reveal order, hierarchy, and assembly.

Compare hybrids formed across species, objects, landscapes, and machines, including gradual metamorphosis and double-reading images.

![Animal-plant hybrid — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-27-1.svg)

##### Animal-plant hybrid

Blend animal structure with leaves, roots, bark, or blossoms as continuous living anatomy.

<a id="form-art-27-2"></a>

![Organic-mechanical hybrid — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-27-2.svg)

<a id="form-art-27-3"></a>

![Object-building hybrid — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-27-3.svg)

Merge a familiar object with architectural massing, turning openings and components into inhabitable structure.

##### Body-landscape hybrid

Let bodily contours become hills, rivers, or terrain while preserving a second figurative reading.

<a id="form-art-27-5"></a>

Attach an alien local feature as if it has grown naturally from the host form.

<a id="form-art-27-6"></a>

![Double image — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-27-6.svg)

Construct one image that resolves into a different subject when attention shifts between shapes.

![Gradual metamorphosis — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-27-7.svg)

##### Gradual metamorphosis

Show one entity transitioning step by step into another through continuous intermediate anatomy.

<a id="form-art-27-8"></a>

![World within a silhouette — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-27-8.svg)

##### World within a silhouette

Use an outer silhouette as a frame for a complete miniature scene inside it.

<a id="form-art-27-9"></a>

![Hybrid creature — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-27-9.svg)

##### Hybrid creature

Invent a creature from several species, unifying mismatched parts through consistent proportion and surface treatment.

#### Figure-ground and illusions

Compare ambiguous boundaries and spatial paradoxes where figure, ground, depth, scale, or dimensionality can reverse.

<a id="form-art-29-0"></a>

![Positive-negative exchange — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/art-29-0.svg)

##### Positive-negative exchange
