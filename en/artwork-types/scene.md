# Scenes

A setting, a moment or a shared space gives the subjects context. Action is optional.

<a id="scene-design"></a>

## Scene composition and treatments

<a id="forms"></a>

<a id="scene"></a>

VISUAL FORMS · 60

### Scene content

<a id="scene-panel-word-10-0"></a>

<a id="scene-group-word-10-0"></a>

#### Events and narrative states

Compare narrative states within a scene, from isolated moments and actions to causes, aftermaths, routines, conflict, and transformation.

<a id="form-exp-10-0-0"></a>

![Single event — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-0-0.svg)

##### Single event

Concentrate the image on one event visibly taking place in the present moment.

<a id="form-exp-10-0-1"></a>

![Action instant — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-0-1.svg)

##### Action instant

Capture one decisive pose, such as jumping, turning back, or catching a ball.

<a id="form-exp-10-0-2"></a>

![Before the event — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-0-2.svg)

##### Before the event

Use preparatory gestures and environmental clues to suggest what is about to happen.

<a id="form-exp-10-0-3"></a>

![After the event — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-0-3.svg)

##### After the event

Show the outcome through remains, facial expressions, or visible changes in the surroundings.

<a id="form-exp-10-0-4"></a>

![Everyday activity — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-0-4.svg)

##### Everyday activity

Depict an ongoing everyday activity such as eating, reading, or walking.

<a id="form-exp-10-0-5"></a>

![Meeting and reunion — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-0-5.svg)

##### Meeting and reunion

Establish a relationship through the characters’ distance, gazes, and poses.

<a id="form-exp-10-0-6"></a>

![Shared work — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-0-6.svg)

##### Shared work

Have several characters divide tasks while working toward one shared objective.

<a id="form-exp-10-0-7"></a>

![Conflict and pursuit — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-0-7.svg)

##### Conflict and pursuit

Create tension through confrontation, escape, or pursuit within the scene.

<a id="form-exp-10-0-8"></a>

![Care and companionship — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-0-8.svg)

##### Care and companionship

Express a relationship through caregiving, waiting, or quietly sharing the same space.

<a id="form-exp-10-0-9"></a>

![Ritual and celebration — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-0-9.svg)

##### Ritual and celebration

Gather a group around a birthday, festival, anniversary, or other commemorative occasion.

<a id="form-exp-10-0-10"></a>

![Exploration and discovery — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-0-10.svg)

##### Exploration and discovery

Show characters entering an unfamiliar place or discovering a new object.

<a id="form-exp-10-0-11"></a>

![Travel and migration — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-0-11.svg)

##### Travel and migration

Make the road, traveling group, and destination together define the journey.

<a id="form-exp-10-0-12"></a>

![Growth and change — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-0-12.svg)

##### Growth and change

Use successive stages of the same subject’s change as the main content.

<a id="form-exp-10-0-13"></a>

![Cyclical routine — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-0-13.svg)

##### Cyclical routine

Suggest cyclical life through repeated actions or recurring changes in the environment.

<a id="form-exp-10-0-14"></a>

![Quiet situation — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-0-14.svg)

##### Quiet situation

Describe a state through subjects and surroundings without showing an obvious event.

<a id="form-exp-10-0-15"></a>

![Unresolved clue — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-0-15.svg)

##### Unresolved clue

Keep the crucial cause or result outside the frame so visible clues invite interpretation.

#### Settings and everyday fragments

Compare everyday settings by social use, objects, occupants, and the kind of ordinary activity each space supports.

<a id="form-exp-10-1-0"></a>

![Domestic interior — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-1-0.svg)

##### Domestic interior

Combine furniture, everyday possessions, and characters to establish a lived-in home interior.

<a id="form-exp-10-1-1"></a>

![Desktop still life — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-1-1.svg)

##### Desktop still life

Suggest daily life through tabletop objects, signs of use, and a localized patch of light.

<a id="form-exp-10-1-2"></a>

![Window corner — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-1-2.svg)

##### Window corner

Use a window, sightline, and the relation between inside and outside to create quietness.

<a id="form-exp-10-1-3"></a>

![Workspace and studio — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-1-3.svg)

##### Workspace and studio

Let tools, materials, and visible working gestures explain the activity.

<a id="form-exp-10-1-4"></a>

![Study and reading space — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-1-4.svg)

##### Study and reading space

Build a learning environment from desks, bookshelves, or classroom relationships.

<a id="form-exp-10-1-5"></a>

![Shop and market — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-1-5.svg)

##### Shop and market

Organize stalls, goods, and exchanges between people into a market scene.

<a id="form-exp-10-1-6"></a>

![Dining and gathering — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-1-6.svg)

##### Dining and gathering

Use a dining table, food, seating, and conversation to create a shared gathering space.

<a id="form-exp-10-1-7"></a>

![Street and corner — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-1-7.svg)

##### Street and corner

Combine building fronts, roads, and pedestrians to portray everyday urban life.

<a id="form-exp-10-1-8"></a>

![Transport and station — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-1-8.svg)

##### Transport and station

Set vehicles and platforms around characters who are waiting or moving through a station.

<a id="form-exp-10-1-9"></a>

![Sport and play — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-1-9.svg)

##### Sport and play

Bring facilities, equipment, and active characters together in a sport or play scene.

<a id="form-exp-10-1-10"></a>

![Garden and courtyard — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-1-10.svg)

##### Garden and courtyard

Use plants, walls, paths, and resting areas to shape an outdoor domestic space.

<a id="form-exp-10-1-11"></a>

![Farm and field — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-1-11.svg)

##### Farm and field

Combine cultivation, harvest activity, animals, and fields in one agricultural scene.

<a id="form-exp-10-1-12"></a>

![Camping and outdoor rest — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-1-12.svg)

##### Camping and outdoor rest

Define an outdoor stopping place with a tent, fire, or picnic equipment.

<a id="form-exp-10-1-13"></a>

![Stage and performance — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-1-13.svg)

##### Stage and performance

Use a performance area, audience, and lighting to establish the act of watching.

<a id="form-exp-10-1-14"></a>

![Workshop and production — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-1-14.svg)

##### Workshop and production

Show workstations, machinery, raw materials, and character actions as one production process.

<a id="form-exp-10-1-15"></a>

![Abandoned space — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-1-15.svg)

##### Abandoned space

Use abandoned objects and traces of time to imply that people were once present.

<a id="form-exp-10-1-16"></a>

![Microhabitat — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-1-16.svg)

##### Microhabitat

Turn a patch of moss, tree hollow, or flower bed into a complete miniature world.

<a id="form-exp-10-1-17"></a>

![Imagined everyday space — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-1-17.svg)

##### Imagined everyday space

Create a home or activity space with everyday functions governed by fantastical rules.

#### Natural and built spaces

Compare natural and built environments by terrain, enclosure, scale, circulation, architectural type, and relationship to water or vegetation.

<a id="form-exp-10-2-0"></a>

![Mountains and canyons — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-2-0.svg)

##### Mountains and canyons

Build the main space from mountain masses, valleys, and strong changes in elevation.

<a id="form-exp-10-2-1"></a>

![Forest interior — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-2-1.svg)

##### Forest interior

Layer trunks, canopy, clearings, and forest floor to create depth inside the woods.

<a id="form-exp-10-2-2"></a>

![Grassland and open country — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-2-2.svg)

##### Grassland and open country

Use low open land beneath a broad sky to suggest a large-scale landscape.

<a id="form-exp-10-2-3"></a>

![Rivers and lakes — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-2-3.svg)

##### Rivers and lakes

Organize the natural setting through shorelines, water flow, or reflections.

<a id="form-exp-10-2-4"></a>

![Coast and sea — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-2-4.svg)

##### Coast and sea

Establish the coast through shoreline contours, tides, rocks, or boats.

<a id="form-exp-10-2-5"></a>

![Underwater world — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-2-5.svg)

##### Underwater world

Use the water body, suspended particles, and marine life to create underwater depth.

<a id="form-exp-10-2-6"></a>

![Desert and wasteland — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-2-6.svg)

##### Desert and wasteland

Shape the space with dunes, exposed ground, and isolated landmarks.

<a id="form-exp-10-2-7"></a>

![Ice and polar landscape — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-2-7.svg)

##### Ice and polar landscape

Organize the environment through ice sheets, snowfields, and cold reflected light.

<a id="form-exp-10-2-8"></a>

![Cave and underground — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-2-8.svg)

##### Cave and underground

Create an enclosed underground space from rock walls, openings, and localized lighting.

<a id="form-exp-10-2-9"></a>

![Sky and clouds — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-2-9.svg)

##### Sky and clouds

Use cloud layers, flying subjects, or suspended structures to form an aerial environment.

<a id="form-exp-10-2-10"></a>

![Cosmos and planets — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-2-10.svg)

##### Cosmos and planets

Make celestial bodies, orbital paths, and cosmic scale the principal content.

<a id="form-exp-10-2-11"></a>

![Building exterior — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-2-11.svg)

##### Building exterior

Show the building’s mass, facade, and surrounding outdoor space together.

<a id="form-exp-10-2-12"></a>

![Building interior — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-2-12.svg)

##### Building interior

Construct the interior from walls, openings, furnishings, and paths through the room.

<a id="form-exp-10-2-13"></a>

![City skyline — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-2-13.svg)

##### City skyline

Unify a skyline through the height rhythm of buildings against the sky.

<a id="form-exp-10-2-14"></a>

![Historic ruins — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-2-14.svg)

##### Historic ruins

Combine surviving architecture, its surroundings, and traces of time to define a historic place.

<a id="form-exp-10-2-15"></a>

![Invented world — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-2-15.svg)

##### Invented world

Organize imaginary terrain or architecture with consistent internal spatial rules.

#### Time and atmosphere

Compare the same scene under different times, seasons, weather, visibility, and atmospheric conditions rather than changing its subject.

<a id="form-exp-10-3-0"></a>

![Early morning — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-3-0.svg)

##### Early morning

Use low, soft light and waking activity to convey the beginning of the day.

<a id="form-exp-10-3-1"></a>

![Bright midday — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-3-1.svg)

##### Bright midday

Show bright daytime through a high sun and clearly defined light and shadow.

<a id="form-exp-10-3-2"></a>

![Dusk — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-3-2.svg)

##### Dusk

Blend low warm light with a darkening environment to mark the transition into evening.

<a id="form-exp-10-3-3"></a>

![Night lights — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-3-3.svg)

##### Night lights

Organize the night scene through artificial lights set against dark spatial zones.

<a id="form-exp-10-3-4"></a>

![Moonlit quiet — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-3-4.svg)

##### Moonlit quiet

Let moonlight and low illumination dominate the mood and visibility of the scene.

<a id="form-exp-10-3-5"></a>

![Rainy scene — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-3-5.svg)

##### Rainy scene

Combine falling rain, wet traces, umbrellas, and reflections to make the weather evident.

<a id="form-exp-10-3-6"></a>

![Snowy scene — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-3-6.svg)

##### Snowy scene

Use falling snow, accumulated cover, and obscured surfaces to transform the space.

<a id="form-exp-10-3-7"></a>

![Foggy scene — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-3-7.svg)

##### Foggy scene

Lower distant contrast and soften spatial boundaries beneath layers of fog.

<a id="form-exp-10-3-8"></a>

![Windy scene — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-3-8.svg)

##### Windy scene

Align plants, clothing, and moving objects to reveal one consistent wind direction.

<a id="form-exp-10-3-9"></a>

![Seasonal comparison — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-3-9.svg)

##### Seasonal comparison

Show one place across seasons by changing vegetation, weather, and light.

#### Viewpoints

Compare camera or observer position through height, tilt, distance, subjectivity, framing devices, section views, and multiple viewpoints.

<a id="form-exp-10-4-0"></a>

![Eye-level view — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-4-0.svg)

##### Eye-level view

Set the viewpoint near the height of the characters or primary objects.

<a id="form-exp-10-4-1"></a>

![Low-angle view — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-4-1.svg)

##### Low-angle view

Look upward from below to strengthen the subject’s apparent mass or height.

<a id="form-exp-10-4-2"></a>

![High-angle view — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-4-2.svg)

##### High-angle view

Look down from an elevated position to reveal relationships across the ground.

<a id="form-exp-10-4-3"></a>

![Top-down view — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-4-3.svg)

##### Top-down view

Look almost straight down so the flat spatial layout becomes the main information.

<a id="form-exp-10-4-4"></a>

![Oblique aerial view — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-4-4.svg)

##### Oblique aerial view

View the scene diagonally from above, showing both ground distribution and three-dimensional volume.

<a id="form-exp-10-4-5"></a>

![Ground-level view — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-4-5.svg)

##### Ground-level view

Place the viewpoint against the ground so even small objects appear tall.

<a id="form-exp-10-4-6"></a>

![Isometric view — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-4-6.svg)

##### Isometric view

Use nearly parallel spatial axes to show a three-dimensional arrangement without strong convergence.

<a id="form-exp-10-4-7"></a>

![One-point perspective — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-4-7.svg)

##### One-point perspective

Direct the spatial lines toward one dominant vanishing point.

<a id="form-exp-10-4-8"></a>

![Two-point perspective — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-4-8.svg)

##### Two-point perspective

Use a building or spatial corner to establish two separate vanishing directions.

<a id="form-exp-10-4-9"></a>

![Three-point perspective — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-4-9.svg)

##### Three-point perspective

Converge the vertical dimension as well, intensifying the upward or downward view.

<a id="form-exp-10-4-10"></a>

![First-person view — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-4-10.svg)

##### First-person view

Frame only the area the character would see directly through their own eyes.

<a id="form-exp-10-4-11"></a>

![Over-the-shoulder view — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-4-11.svg)

##### Over-the-shoulder view

Keep a character’s shoulder and back in the foreground while looking toward the event.

<a id="form-exp-10-4-12"></a>

![View through an opening — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-4-12.svg)

##### View through an opening

View another space through a door, window, hole, or similar opening.

<a id="form-exp-10-4-13"></a>

![Distant observer — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-4-13.svg)

##### Distant observer

Observe the event from a distance so its activity reads within the larger environment.

<a id="form-exp-10-4-14"></a>

![Close participant — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-4-14.svg)

##### Close participant

Place the viewpoint among the characters to create a stronger sense of participation.

<a id="form-exp-10-4-15"></a>

![Surface-level view — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-4-15.svg)

##### Surface-level view

Set the viewpoint close to a natural surface to emphasize the spaces above and below it.

<a id="form-exp-10-4-16"></a>

![Cutaway view — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-4-16.svg)

##### Cutaway view

Remove obstructing surfaces to reveal the interior of a building or environment.

<a id="form-exp-10-4-17"></a>

![Multiple viewpoints — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-4-17.svg)

##### Multiple viewpoints

Combine different viewing directions within one scene and make their joins explicit.

<a id="scene-composition-panel-word-10-5"></a>

<a id="scene-composition-group-word-10-5"></a>

#### Framing range

Compare framing range by how much environment and subject detail remain visible, from extreme wides to intimate close-ups.

<a id="form-exp-10-5-0"></a>

![Extreme wide shot — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-5-0.svg)

##### Extreme wide shot

Keep people very small while allowing the broad environment to dominate.

<a id="form-exp-10-5-1"></a>

![Wide scene — visual study](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/exp-10-5-1.svg)

##### Wide scene

AI DIRECTIONS

## Take this direction into AI

Use with an image, or ask AI to rewrite your image prompt in this direction.

**Target direction**

Scenes

**Input and output**

If given an image, adapt the image to this direction. If given a prompt, rewrite it as a complete prompt ready for image generation.

**Target structure**

Organize subjects, setting and space around the original theme. The result may be an event, a quiet setting, a landscape or an everyday vignette; create related surroundings if they are absent.

**Background**

Use existing location, environmental and atmospheric cues to develop a coherent setting. If no background exists, or only a solid or transparent backing is supplied, create a related environment. A decorative pattern alone must not replace essential context.

**Wording**

Keep clearly readable source wording and its language, adapting its presentation. Do not claim to have recovered unreadable text. If no words are supplied, create a brief related phrase as supporting copy without obscuring key subjects, action or structure. Follow the target hierarchy for existing wording and respect an explicit request for no text.

**Preserve and adapt**

Keep the core theme and identifying features. Use the source treatment and palette as a starting point, changing them only as required by the selected direction. Do not describe newly created content as present in the source. State a conflict between explicit input restrictions and necessary target conditions rather than claiming both were met.

**Scope and boundaries**

Action is not required. Decoration behind an isolated object without contextual relationships is closer to subject-led artwork.

Code-drawn studies compare composition and visual treatments; media and style examples are conceptual. These directions have not been individually tested with image models. Check generated text, transparency and repeat edges.
