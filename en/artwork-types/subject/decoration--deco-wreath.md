# Surrounding decoration

Decorative elements form a partial or complete enclosure around the subject.

<a id="understand"></a>

![Surrounding decoration: Decorative elements form a partial or complete enclosure around the subject.](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/legacy-decoration--deco-wreath-1.svg)

Composition illustration

HOW TO RECOGNIZE IT

## What defines this direction

Symmetry and a closed ring are optional. A patch mainly behind the subject is a local patterned backdrop; the two approaches may be combined.

The treatment works around one subject or an identifiable group.

AI DIRECTIONS

## Take this direction into AI

Use with an image, or ask AI to rewrite your image prompt in this direction.

**Target direction**

Subject-led → Shared decoration → Surrounding decoration

**Input and output**

If given an image, adapt the image to this direction. If given a prompt, rewrite it as a complete prompt ready for image generation.

**Target structure**

Arrange related branches, lines, symbols or motifs mainly around the outside of the subject or grouping. Build a partial ring, wreath or free enclosure with the subject as its focal point.

**Background**

Reference existing background content, treatment and palette, adapting it to the target structure. If absent or only plain or transparent, create theme-related background treatment. An explicit solid, transparent or other background requirement takes precedence.

**Wording**

Keep clearly readable source wording and its language, adapting its presentation. Do not claim to have recovered unreadable text. If no words are supplied, create a brief related phrase as supporting copy without obscuring key subjects, action or structure. Follow the target hierarchy for existing wording and respect an explicit request for no text.

**Preserve and adapt**

Keep the core theme and identifying features. Use the source treatment and palette as a starting point, changing them only as required by the selected direction. Do not describe newly created content as present in the source. State a conflict between explicit input restrictions and necessary target conditions rather than claiming both were met.

**Scope and boundaries**

Symmetry and a closed ring are optional. A patch mainly behind the subject is a local patterned backdrop; the two approaches may be combined.
