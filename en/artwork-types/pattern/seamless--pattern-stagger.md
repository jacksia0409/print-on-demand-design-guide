# Offset repeat

Adjacent rows or columns shift by a consistent amount within a repeating system.

HOW TO RECOGNIZE IT

## What defines this direction

Offsets may vary by design but require a stable overall period. Moving a few individual motifs does not establish seamless tiling.

AI DIRECTIONS

## Take this direction into AI

Use with an image, or ask AI to rewrite your image prompt in this direction.

**Target direction**

Patterns → Seamless repeats → Offset repeat

**Input and output**

If given an image, adapt the image to this direction. If given a prompt, rewrite it as a complete prompt ready for image generation.

**Target structure**

Build a tile containing a stable offset relationship between adjacent rows or columns. Coordinate motif direction, spacing and density, including opposite-edge continuation.

**Background**

Reference background palette and motifs, designing background and foreground inside the same repeat unit. When absent, create a related base color or a backing that can continue periodically. Rebuild a nonmatching source background rather than claiming the complete image tiles automatically.

**Wording**

Keep clearly readable source wording and its language, adapting its presentation. Do not claim to have recovered unreadable text. If no words are supplied, create a short theme-related word or phrase as a supporting motif. When seamless tiling is required, include wording in the repeat unit and check spelling, edge continuation and readability; otherwise do not promise seamlessness. Respect an explicit request for no text.

**Preserve and adapt**

Keep the core theme and identifying features. Use the source treatment and palette as a starting point, changing them only as required by the selected direction. Do not describe newly created content as present in the source. State a conflict between explicit input restrictions and necessary target conditions rather than claiming both were met.

**Scope and boundaries**

Offsets may vary by design but require a stable overall period. Moving a few individual motifs does not establish seamless tiling.

**Output check**

Test horizontal, vertical and four-tile arrangements for seams, rhythm and base-color continuity. Do not claim verified seamlessness without checking or when the tool cannot ensure it.
