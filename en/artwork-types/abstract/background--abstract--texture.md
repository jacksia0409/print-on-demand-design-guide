# Textured background

Abstract artwork: Paper grain, particles or another surface character support the foreground content.

<a id="understand"></a>

![Textured background: Abstract artwork: Paper grain, particles or another surface character support the foreground content.](https://images.podplo.app/official/pod-guide/diagrams/b2382e28cd57b514819e/legacy-background--abstract--texture-1.svg)

Composition illustration

HOW TO RECOGNIZE IT

## What defines this direction

A textured backing should not automatically become an independent object or a whole narrative scene. In the texture category, distinguish the main surface fragment from its supporting ground.

AI DIRECTIONS

## Take this direction into AI

Use with an image, or ask AI to rewrite your image prompt in this direction.

**Target direction**

Abstract artwork → Background → Textured background

**Input and output**

If given an image, adapt the image to this direction. If given a prompt, rewrite it as a complete prompt ready for image generation.

**Target structure**

Retain nonfigurative form, line and color relationships. The ground should support the abstraction rather than introduce a dominant identifiable object.

**Background**

Reference existing surface detail, palette and density, or create a theme-related low-interference texture when absent. Coordinate scale and contrast so the material stays in a supporting role.

**Wording**

Keep clearly readable source wording and its language, adapting its presentation. Do not claim to have recovered unreadable text. If no words are supplied, create a brief related phrase as supporting copy without obscuring key subjects, action or structure. Follow the target hierarchy for existing wording and respect an explicit request for no text.

**Preserve and adapt**

Retain theme, atmosphere, palette, rhythm or direction while allowing concrete object identity to be reduced or removed. Do not simultaneously require the original object silhouette to remain intact. Do not describe newly created content as present in the source. State a conflict between explicit input restrictions and necessary target conditions rather than claiming both were met.

**Scope and boundaries**

A textured backing should not automatically become an independent object or a whole narrative scene. In the texture category, distinguish the main surface fragment from its supporting ground.
