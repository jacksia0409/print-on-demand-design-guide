## Restore a damaged memory photo

[Detail Repair](https://podplo.app/skills/detail-repair) fixes damage in an old photo while preserving familiar people and objects.

A faded family, travel, wedding, or archival photo may be the only record of an important moment. [Detail Repair](https://podplo.app/skills/detail-repair) can fix scratches, blurry areas, or damaged objects while preserving familiar faces and key details.
