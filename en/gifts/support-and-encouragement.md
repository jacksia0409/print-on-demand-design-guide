# Support and encouragement

Use a familiar photo or short personal message to create a thoughtful design for a difficult moment or new beginning.

<a id="courage"></a>

## Build the design around an encouraging line

[Compact Center Layout](https://podplo.app/skills/compact-center-layout) gives an encouraging short phrase a clear, steady presence.

Use a phrase the recipient already values for an exam, challenge, or new beginning. [Compact Center Layout](https://podplo.app/skills/compact-center-layout) gives the line a clear, steady presence without turning it into a promise about the outcome.

## Pair a name with an honest message

[Floral Name Frame](https://podplo.app/skills/floral-name-frame) pairs a name with a direct, personal message.

A name and a direct line such as ‘thinking of you’ can become a personal floral design. [Floral Name Frame](https://podplo.app/skills/floral-name-frame) presents the message with care without trying to replace the conversation behind it.
